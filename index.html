How it works

Three steps

1

You choose the apps

Pick games and videos in Apple's own app list. Waddles never sees which apps you chose.

2

Your child does a quest

A few right answers in a row (you choose 2 to 5). A miss just starts a fresh row: no hearts, no penalties.

3

The apps open for a while

15 to 60 minutes, your choice. When time is up, iOS locks them again, even if Waddles is closed.

For parents

You stay in charge

Your child's iPhone

Approved by a parent through Family Sharing. Protection is always on.

Or lend your own iPhone

Start sharing before you hand it over; your PIN ends it. Choose just some apps, or everything except the apps you approve, like school apps.

  • Settings sit behind a parent PIN, with a backup code if you forget it.
  • App choices, your PIN and quest progress stay on the iPhone.
  • No ads and no tracking in the app.

Waddles makes skipping a quest harder, not impossible. On your own iPhone, iOS always lets you, the owner, turn off Waddles' Screen Time access in Settings.
