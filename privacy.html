Privacy policy

This policy covers the Waddles iPhone app and this website. "We" means Arian Sarathy, who makes Waddles. Questions: waddles.app.help@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Waddles works on your iPhone. App choices, your PIN, quest progress and settings stay there. We never receive them.
  • Apple shows Waddles the apps you choose only as hidden tokens, so Waddles can't see which apps or websites they are, or how long anyone uses them.
  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics in the app. No account is needed.
  • Waddles Pro is bought through Apple. Only when a parent opens the Parent Vault does Waddles ask our subscription provider, RevenueCat, whether Pro is active, using an anonymous ID.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

What stays on your iPhone

These are stored on the device and are not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • The apps, categories and websites you choose to lock or to approve. iOS gives Waddles these only as tokens it can't read.
  • Settings: app time per quest, right answers in a row, difficulty, subjects, protection mode and whether your iPhone is being shared.
  • Quest progress: how many quests were finished, the adventure board's level, and a short learning history for the parent's weekly summary: for each day, how many answers were right or wrong in each subject, and which sums or times tables were missed. The history covers the last 14 days; older days are deleted automatically. It never leaves the iPhone.
  • Your parent PIN and backup code. Waddles keeps only a slow, salted hash of each, in the iPhone's Keychain, set so they never move to another device. Neither can be read back, by us or by anyone. If you open the Parent Vault with Face ID on your own iPhone, iOS tells Waddles only whether it matched; Waddles never sees your face. It keeps a short value, also in the Keychain, that changes when faces are added or removed, so it can ask for your PIN after a change.

Screen Time

Waddles uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to lock the apps you choose and to open them for a while after a quest. A parent approves this through Family Sharing, or the iPhone's owner approves it with Face ID or the passcode. Waddles doesn't receive app names, usage times or browsing history, and it doesn't make usage reports.

Purchases (Waddles Pro)

Subscriptions are bought through Apple. We don't receive your card details or your Apple Account details.

To know whether Waddles Pro is active, Waddles uses RevenueCat, our subscription provider. RevenueCat receives a random ID made on your iPhone (not your name or email), the purchase information from Apple, and basic technical details such as the app version, your country or region, currency and the IP address of your connection. It uses them to confirm the subscription and to give us totals such as how many people subscribe. Waddles doesn't link this to who you are, and it isn't used for advertising.

Waddles contacts RevenueCat only after a parent opens the Parent Vault, where the plans are. From then until Waddles is closed, RevenueCat's software may check again when Waddles comes back to the screen. The rest of the time, including during a child's quests, Waddles uses what it last learned, stored on the iPhone.

Children

Waddles is set up by a parent and used by a child. We don't collect personal information from children: a child's answers and progress stay on the iPhone, and the app has no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking. The only thing that leaves the iPhone, a subscription check, happens when a parent opens the Parent Vault, not because of anything the child does.

This website

The website has no account and no forms. Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security, for a short time.

When we advertise Waddles through Whop Ads, this website uses the Whop Pixel to count visits from those ads and taps on the App Store button. It sets a first-party cookie named _wuid. It runs on the website only, never in the app, and it can't see anything you do in Waddles. You can block or delete this cookie in your browser settings at any time.

Who we share information with

We don't sell personal information or share it for advertising. The only other parties involved are the service providers above, each for the purpose described: Apple (Screen Time and payments), RevenueCat (subscription status), Whop (website ad measurement) and our web host. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

  • On your iPhone: the learning history is kept for 14 days. Deleting Waddles removes all of its data. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so Waddles erases its PIN and backup code the next time it's installed.
  • Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the subscription and for accounting. Cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to how it's used. Email waddles.app.help@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days. Most of what Waddles handles never reaches us, so there's often nothing on our side to delete.

Security

The PIN and backup code are stored only as slow, salted hashes in the iPhone's Keychain. Anything that leaves the device is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your iPhone, we'll say so in the app before it applies.
